# Anatomical Image Dataset & Reference/Review Pairing Inventory

This document tracks the verified open-source photographic reference images and their corresponding controlled review derivatives for the Body Check application.

All reference photographs have been researched, verified, and processed according to clinical examination standards:
- Realistic clinical/examination-style photography
- Neutral lighting and plain, distraction-free backgrounds
- Healthy, intact, uninjured normal skin (no abrasions, bruises, surgical scars, or tattoos)
- Anonymity preserved (no visible patient faces, medical charts, wristbands, or identifiable markers)
- Permissive Creative Commons / Open Access licensing with exact attribution
